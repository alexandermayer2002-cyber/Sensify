### 2 · HIGH — Day 4 with a real 3-day gap: nothing anywhere says what blank days do to the protocol
The screen that greets me: streak ring "–", "STREAK STARTS WITH TONIGHT'S CHECK-IN", and the
DAYS 1–4 strip showing Sa – / Su – / Mo – (three "No check-in" dashes).
Believed (in persona, after reading the legend twice): my first week is damaged. The legend's own
words: "The day went unlogged with no explanation, so there is no data and no word. This is the
one that resets your streak. **A slip we can work with. A blank we cannot.**"
What I could NOT find on any screen: whether the 56-day clock kept running, whether my baseline
is ruined, whether day 56 moved, whether I should restart, or any way to repair the blanks. The
legend describes an "○ Away, on plan" state ("when you returned you told us you stayed on your
plan") — but no door to it exists anywhere I can reach: no welcome-back prompt fired on my return
(3-day gap is evidently below the away-gate threshold), the daily check-in asks only about today,
and History has no editable past. The product names a state I cannot enter, right under three
dashes it told me it "cannot work with."
What was actually true (confirmed only by interrogating Ask Sensify): nothing resets; the clock
runs; blanks cost streak + data only. A real tester who misses days 1–3 — the single most likely
week-1 failure — is left to conclude the worst on their own. Stall/quit risk is real; this is
also the first live observation of the real-gap experience the fleet can't simulate.

### 3 · MED — "Tonight" vs "today": the product never says WHEN a check-in should happen
Copy collisions, all on one morning screen: hero "STREAK STARTS WITH **TONIGHT'S** CHECK-IN";
first-week guide "Ten seconds **each night**: did you stick to it, how you slept, how you feel.
**Tonight counts.**"; but the big button is "**Today's** check-in", live at 9am, and the form asks
"Did you stay on your plan **today**?" — about a day that hasn't happened yet.
Believed (literally): check-ins are a night ritual, so tapping at 9am is either not allowed or
dishonest — if I answer "Yes, on plan" now and slip at dinner, I've lied to my own record.
True: the form submits any time, counts for today, and nothing says whether it can be amended.
An anxious reader either waits all day (fine) or morning-logs and worries (also fine) — but the
product forced the reader to invent the rule. One sentence ("check in any time; it counts for
today and you can update it until midnight" — or whatever is true) would end it.

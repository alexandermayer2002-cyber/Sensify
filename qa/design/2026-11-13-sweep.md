## Ready to ship?

Not as a set. Home (every state I could render), the check-in and the payoff are consistent and could go. The Reintro tab and the Verdicts cover would be the first things a person sees after they leave Home in the first week, and both still look like the old product. Ship order that works: fix rows 1 to 4 (tab rebuild, italics, one forest panel on complete, one clock per trial), then rows 5 and 8 so the same food reads the same on both screens, then ship.

## Make it better (tied to the person's question)

1. "Where am I in this trial?" Give the whole product one sentence for it, built once from the log, and let every screen print exactly that: "Exposure day 2 of 3" or "Washout day 5 of 11".
2. "Did I eat it?" On the check-in, show yesterday's answer under the question so a skipped night is visible before it is logged twice.
3. "What does my ruling rest on?" On Verdicts, tap a card to open the strip of the actual nights, not a summary; the evidence line should count logged nights.
4. "What do I tell the person cooking?" Make the household card the only dark panel on every Home and name the trial food in plain words, never "this week".
5. "Did it help?" Move the outcome question into the Home complete card as a paper object, one tap, and echo the answer on Verdicts.

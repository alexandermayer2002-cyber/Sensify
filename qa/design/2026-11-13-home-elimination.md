## Make it better

1. **Day 1 and early ("What do I do today?" / "Am I doing it right?")**: lead with the strip filling in. After a log, say "Day three. Logged." and let the strip show tonight as on plan, so the answer to "did it count" is a drawn square, not a sentence.
2. **Mid ("Is it working?")**: show the ledger as the one hero, once-captioned, and add the one-line baseline sharpness under it with real counts. Collapse suspects to one sentence so the weekly scores answer the question.
3. **Late ("When does testing start, in what order?")**: make the ordered suspect cards the hero, with the day-57 line directly above them: "Testing begins on day 57. Your order is set." Put the strip and ledger below.
4. **Weekly due ("Is it open, did I miss it?")**: use the `catchup` label when late and say so in the headline, with the nightly link kept quiet so there is one action.
5. **Streak ("Am I on track?")**: use the broken and away streak lines so a person with a gap is told "Streak reset last night. Tonight starts a new one." and an away person is told "Your streak is paused, not broken."

# Ship list: branch redesign/objects → main

Prepared 2026-11-13. Nothing here is live until Alex says ship. One merge,
one Netlify build.

## What changes for a customer

**Shell.** The app sits on a stone surface instead of cream; the nav is
quieter (no phase pill during the protocol); the mono caps label style is
gone from every screen.

**Home, during elimination.** Rebuilt from the seven shared objects. One
question per stretch: days 1 to 7 "one job, log tonight" with the strip as
hero; days 8 to 35 "your record is building" with weekly scores against the
day-zero baseline, baseline sharpness, the strip; days 36 to 56 "testing
begins on day 57, your order is set" with the ordered suspect cards as hero.
No projected trial dates. Streak as a sentence. Household card is a dark
panel with the foods as chips and no raw URL.

**Home, during reintroduction and at complete.** States: trial starts
tomorrow, exposure (eat it today / logged), washout (day n of eleven),
verdict ready (one button), stalled trial (restart, no blame), between
cycles (pick your next food), complete (Food Map, outcome question). Cycle
strip drawn from the trial's own logs; clock matches the Reintro tab. Food
Map cards: ruled (verdict color), on trial, next, suspect; evidence lines
read from the logs. A retest never hides the standing verdict.

**Reintro tab.** Header answers the state. Pre-57: "Testing opens on day
57", how a trial works (shared strip), your order (suspect cards). Active
trial: one paper card with the strip, one instruction, one button. Briefing
only during exposure. No projected "verdict by" dates. Tested ledger in
sentence case. No italics, arrows or em-dashes.

**Nightly check-in.** Serif questions, larger targets, "Done for today"
unchanged. Severe reaction panel leads with "If this feels serious, get
medical care first" and is not red before a ruling. Payoff: "Logged for
tonight", the week as the shared strip with tonight filled, streak line,
"Back to Home".

**Verdicts.** The shared verdict and suspect cards on the stone page; no
tinted washes; header in sentence case; day-zero echo no longer italic.

**Quick wins.** Verdict reveal counts logged days instead of "Day 14 of
14". Survey no longer says "During the 1 days after". Program-complete
summary told never to invent a duration ("six months"). Hero clock labels
never claim "map complete". PDF header only counts days at a full trial.
Evidence lines never claim 14 days unless 14 were logged, and never invent
a serving threshold.

## What does NOT change

Logic of verdicts, cycles, check-ins, intake, lab, payments. Marketing
pages. History, Ask Sensify, Maintain, Intake, Lab entry, Weekly check-in
and the Household public page keep their layouts (labels only lost the mono
caps style). Copy that the harness depends on ("Done for today", "Pick your
next food", "Start cycle", "Get my verdict") is unchanged.

# Design review: Home / Reintroduction (exposure, washout, verdict ready, stalled, between cycles) and Program complete, branch redesign/objects

Reviewer: Design Director. Read-only pass, 2026-11-13.
Evidence: screenshots from the local build, desktop 1440 and phone 390: exposure day 1 before log (+robotrx1: home_elim_rx_desk.png, home_elim_rx_phone.png), stalled trial (+robotrw1: home_elim_rw_desk.png), program complete (+fable1: home_elim_f1_desk.png, home_elim_f1_phone.png). Code: src/pages/HomeReintro.js (wired at Dashboard.js:1566), src/utils/evidence.js, src/components/objects/index.js and objects.css, HouseholdCard.js (panel variant, 147-190), and the clock math in ReintroTab.js:765-797 and DailyCheckin.js:218-250. Washout, verdict ready and between cycles were not rendered (the reintro daily check-in robot step fails on "Did you eat", so no exposure logs landed). Every finding about those states is marked "code".

**Verdict: FIX FIRST. The two rendered states are calm, the verdict colors stay on ruled cards only, and the Food Map reads well, but the cycle clock does not agree with the Reintro tab or with itself, the strip draws a logged night as "no check-in", and the page can announce "Testing is open. Choose your first food." to someone who has three verdicts.**

Checked and fine: the clock constants (3, 11, 5) and the `cycleDay`, `inExposure`, `needsRestart` and `isVerdictDay` tests match ReintroTab.js:765-797 line for line; the stalled cut (`cycleDay > 5`) matches Dashboard.js:1252. Verdict colors appear only on ruled cards (the rx and rw shots show "On trial." and "Out." in ink; f1 shows "Limit." and "Safe." in color); `VerdictCard state="trial"` carries no verdict class. Ruled cards keep their place in testing order. Unruled cards say "Out." and "Still off your plate. Not yet tried." Between-cycles marks only the first unruled food "Up next." and never when a cycle runs. No projected trial dates, no "MAP COMPLETE", no weekly link on verdict or complete, no streak on complete, no counter on complete. No em-dashes, exclamation marks or middle dots in HomeReintro.js. One dark panel per screen (the household panel). The 5-block limit holds in exposure and washout.

## Make it better

1. **Exposure ("Did I eat <food>? What do I log?")**: let the strip say it. One dark square per logged serving, "Three servings, one a day, then eleven days of watching", and after a log the headline names the count from the same data the strip uses so the two can never differ.
2. **Washout ("Anything to do today?")**: one sentence and the strip: "Keep cow milk out. Day six of eleven." Put exactly the number the Reintro tab shows, and nothing about the verdict day until the last two days.
3. **Verdict day ("Anything to do?")**: hero and one button only. Under the strip print the real count: "Eleven of fourteen nights logged." The person sees why the ruling will be as strong as it is.
4. **Between cycles ("What do I do today?")**: lead with the map so far as the hero (ruled cards first in their ranks) and the single button. Name the next food in the button: "Start the wheat trial".
5. **Program complete ("Am I done?")**: answer "yes" once, show the cards, then ask the one thing SCREENS owes: "Compared to day one, how are your symptoms now?" with a single door. That closes the program and gives the outcome a place on the screen they land on.

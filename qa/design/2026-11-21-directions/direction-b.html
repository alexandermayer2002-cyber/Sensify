Most people suspect food. Almost none ever confirm it.

Bloating, brain fog, reflux, fatigue that keeps coming back. A test can tell you what to look at. Only a record can tell you what is happening.

What a lab test gives you

An IgG test measures how much your immune system has been exposed to a food. It flags what is worth investigating. It cannot tell you what is causing your symptoms.

Each flagged food is removed, then reintroduced alone, while you log how your body responds every night. The ruling comes from your own data.

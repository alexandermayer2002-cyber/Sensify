SMS Messaging & Consent

How Sensify customers opt in to text messages. Last updated June 24, 2026.

This page describes exactly how end users provide consent to receive SMS text messages from Sensify LLC, the messages they receive, and how they opt out. It is provided so the opt-in process can be reviewed without an account.

Business & messaging summary

Sensify is a food sensitivity wellness program. Customers enroll in a structured 6-month program. As part of the program, and only after explicit opt-in, customers receive recurring automated SMS messages: daily check-in reminders asking whether they stayed on their elimination plan (they reply YES or NO), and reintroduction updates related to their personal protocol.

How users opt in (step by step)

  1. A person creates a Sensify account and begins the intake process at our website.
  2. During intake, before the program begins, the user reaches an agreement screen.
  3. On that screen, the user is shown the SMS consent disclosure (reproduced exactly below) as one of several program agreement items.
  4. The user must actively check a consent checkbox that is unchecked by default, and type their name to sign, before they can proceed.
  5. Consent to receive SMS messages is not a condition of purchase. The checkbox requires active, affirmative opt-in.

The exact consent disclosure shown to users

This is the SMS consent item displayed on the intake agreement screen, word for word:

You agree to receive daily SMS check-ins

As part of your program you will receive recurring automated text messages from Sensify, including daily check-in reminders and reintroduction updates. Message and data rates may apply. Consent is not a condition of purchase. Reply STOP at any time to opt out, or HELP for assistance.

And this is the affirmative consent checkbox the user must actively check (shown here in its checked state for illustration; it is unchecked by default in the app):

I understand that Sensify is a wellness program and not a medical service, that insights are for informational purposes only, and that my program data may be accessed by the Sensify team to support my program. I consent to receive recurring automated SMS check-in messages from Sensify and understand I can reply STOP to opt out at any time.

Sample messages

Hi Alex, this is your daily Sensify check-in. Did you stay on your elimination plan today? Reply YES or NO. Reply STOP to opt out.
Hi Alex, your next reintroduction cycle is ready. Open the Sensify app to begin testing your next food. Reply STOP to opt out.

Opt-out and help

Users can opt out of SMS messages at any time by replying STOP, which ends all messages. Replying HELP returns assistance information. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply.

Privacy & data sharing

Sensify does not share mobile information or SMS opt-in data with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Mobile numbers and messaging consent are used solely to deliver the program messages described above. See our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

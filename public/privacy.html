Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 22, 2026

Sensify LLC ("Sensify," "we," "us," or "our") operates the Sensify food sensitivity wellness program. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using Sensify, you agree to the practices described here.

Information We Collect

  • Account information: your name, email address, mobile phone number, and password.
  • Health and program data: symptom reports, baseline assessments, lab test results you upload, daily compliance responses, reintroduction logs, and the food sensitivity verdicts generated during your program.
  • Payment information: processed securely by our payment processor (Stripe). We do not store your full card details.
  • Usage information: basic technical data about how you interact with the app.

How We Use Your Information

  • To deliver and personalize your program, including generating your Food Map and program insights.
  • To send you service communications, including daily SMS check-in reminders and reintroduction updates (see "SMS Messaging" below).
  • To provide customer and protocol support, and to review program quality.
  • To process payments and manage your account.

SMS Messaging

SMS consent and data sharing: When you opt in to receive SMS messages from Sensify, you will receive recurring automated text messages including daily check-in reminders and reintroduction updates. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. You can opt out at any time by replying STOP, and reply HELP for help. Consent to receive SMS messages is not a condition of purchasing any goods or services.

We do not share your mobile information or SMS opt-in data with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Mobile phone numbers and messaging consent are used solely to deliver the Sensify program messages described above.

How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only as follows:

  • Service providers: trusted vendors who help us operate the service (for example, our hosting provider, our payment processor, and our SMS delivery provider), bound by confidentiality obligations.
  • The Sensify team: your program data is accessible to authorized Sensify staff for account verification, protocol support, and quality review.
  • Legal requirements: when required by law or to protect rights, safety, and security.

No mobile information or SMS opt-in data is shared with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

Data Security

We use industry-standard safeguards to protect your information, including encryption in transit and access controls. Uploaded lab files are stored privately and accessed through secure, time-limited links. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to protect your data.

Your Choices and Rights

  • You can opt out of SMS messages at any time by replying STOP.
  • You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data by contacting us.
  • You can close your account at any time.

Medical Disclaimer

Sensify is a wellness and educational program. It is not a medical service and does not diagnose, treat, or cure any condition. Always consult a qualified healthcare provider for medical concerns.

Children's Privacy

Sensify is not intended for individuals under 18, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by the "Last updated" date above.
